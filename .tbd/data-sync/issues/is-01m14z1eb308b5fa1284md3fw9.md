Prerequisite for the convergence fix (sibling bead on owned-field loops). A settled-looking pair that keeps reporting work must be answerable without reading .tbd/data-sync/bridge/ by hand, which is what GH #265 had to do to establish that base, title, status, priority and remote_updated_at all agreed.

Today nothing reports WHY a pair is dirty: the summary gives counts, integration status reports config/credential/reachability only, and skippedPushes is missing from dry runs entirely (sibling bead). The reporter could see 'would push 13' but never learn which field was stuck.

Deliver: a per-pair, per-field dump of local value, remote value, base value, the field_sync rule that applied, and the outcome the matrix reached. This is what identifies the real stuck field if the label hypothesis turns out to be wrong for the general case, which is why it ships before any reconciliation change.
